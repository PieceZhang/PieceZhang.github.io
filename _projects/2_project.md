## SonoPilot and Robotic Ultrasound-Guided Autonomous Needle Insertion

**July 2025 - Present**

SonoPilot is a vision-language-action robotic system for adaptive ultrasound-guided needle insertion and tracking. It closes the loop between ultrasound perception, procedural reasoning, and physical action, with uncertainty-aware slowdown and stopping behavior.

The accompanying RUGANI repository provides a reproducible, Dockerized ROS 2 platform integrating ultrasound imaging, UR5e and Franka Research 3 robot control, NDI optical tracking, force/torque sensing, calibration, and real-time motion planning. My work includes system integration and customization and fine-tuning of Qwen 2.5-VL-series models.
