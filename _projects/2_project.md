## Intelligent VLM/VLA System for Robotic Ultrasound-Guided Needle Intervention

**July 2025 - Present**

This project targets robotic liver ablation through a Dockerized ROS 2 architecture that integrates ultrasound imaging, UR5e and Franka Research 3 dual-arm control, optical tracking, vision-language and vision-language-action models, and real-time motion planning.

The system combines a VLM-based interactive needle-tracking module with a VLA-based adaptive control model for robot-driven ultrasound probe manipulation. My work includes ROS integration and customization and fine-tuning of Qwen 2.5-VL-series models.
