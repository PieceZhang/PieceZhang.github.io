## MrTrack: Register Mamba for Ultrasound Needle Tracking

MrTrack is a Mamba-based tracker designed for needles undergoing rapid back-and-forth motion during ultrasound-guided aspiration biopsy. Its register extract-retrieve paradigm preserves motion-relevant information for robust localization under difficult appearance changes.

The project was accepted early at **MICCAI 2025**. The public repository includes the model implementation, training and evaluation workflows, pretrained weights, and a tracking demo.
