## Motion-Guided Dual-Camera Tracker

MotionDCTrack combines cross-camera mutual templates with Mamba-based motion modeling to improve endoscope tracking under occlusion, illumination changes, and viewpoint variation. The system was evaluated on a mechanical gastric simulator and supports motion analysis for comparing operator skill.

Published at **ICRA 2025**, the open-source release includes training and evaluation code, pretrained weights, and both raw and preprocessed endoscopy datasets.

- [Code, weights, and dataset](https://github.com/PieceZhang/MotionDCTrack)
