## Physics-Based Needle Shape Modeling

NeedleShapeModeling is a research toolkit for estimating needle-tip displacement and visualizing three-dimensional needle deformation under externally applied forces and moments.

It provides a nonlinear SBCM formulation, a distributed-load Euler-Bernoulli beam model, an installation-free browser demonstration, PyQt desktop interfaces, and real-time integration with a Net F/T sensor through the RDT protocol. The toolkit also supports filtering, packet monitoring, and CSV export for experiments.

- [Source code and interactive tools](https://github.com/PieceZhang/NeedleShapeModeling)

> This engineering prototype is intended for research use and has not been validated as a clinical navigation system.
