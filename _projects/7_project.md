## MPT-CataBlur: Microscopy Defocus Deblurring

MPT is a unified framework for microscopy defocus deblurring that combines a multi-pyramid transformer with contrastive learning. The work was published at **CVPR 2024**.

The open-source release provides training and inference code, model weights, and **CataBlur**, a real surgical-microscope dataset containing 1,208 defocused images collected from five cataract surgeries.

- [Code, models, and dataset](https://github.com/PieceZhang/MPT-CataBlur)
